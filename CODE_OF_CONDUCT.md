# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We want a welcoming, practical, and respectful project. We commit to making participation
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and kind.
- Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, then ask.
- Give context when you disagree.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call.
- Help newcomers understand the model.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or imagery.
- Sexualized language or attention.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.
- Bad-faith security reports or doxxing.

## Scope

This applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and any project space.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this policy. Repeated or severe
violations may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

Report concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository’s security contact. Reports
will be reviewed in good faith and kept confidential where possible.

## Attribution

This policy is informed by common open-source community standards, including the Contributor
Covenant.
